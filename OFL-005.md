The first version should help a small-business owner answer a few clear, practical questions about their monthly finances:

How much total revenue did I earn this month?
How much did I spend this month?
What is my net cash flow or profit/loss for the month?
How do my revenue, expenses, and net cash flow compare across different months?
Which expense categories are costing me the most?
How does my actual spending compare with my planned budget for each category?
Which budget categories are over budget, under budget, or on track?
Are there any missing, duplicate, or incorrectly formatted records in my uploaded financial data?
