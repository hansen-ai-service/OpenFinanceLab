# Use Scenarios (Milestone 1)

**Primary User:** Small business owner with transaction data in CSV/spreadsheet form.

## Scenario 1 - Identify Data Problems Before Analysis
- A business owner uploads an expense CSV with duplicate transactions, missing categories, and inconsistent date formats.
**Goal:** Know whether the data can be trusted before using any real numbers
**Outcome:** The platform shows a data-quality summary (rows read, accepted, rejected, and why) before any calculations run.

## Scenario 2 - Understand Monthly Performance
- A business owner uploads a spreadsheet of monthly sales and expenses.
**Goal:** Understand how much revenue the business generated relative to its expenses.
**Outcome:** The platform shows total revenue, total expenses, and net cash flow for the month.

## Scenario 3 - Compare Budget With Actual Spending
- A business owner uploads budget data and actual expense data.
**Goal:** Find which expense categories went over budget.
**Outcome:** The platform compares actual to budgeted amounts and highlights the largest unfavorable variances.

## Scenario 4 - Understand Unusual Changes
- A business owner notices expenses rose in one month and opens the dashboard.
**Goal:** Find out which category caused the increase.
**Outcome:** The platform visualizes the change and identifies which expense categories contributed most to the increase.

## Scenario 5 - Forecast Future Cash Flow (stretch goal)
- A business owner has several months of historical revenue and expense data loaded.
**Goal:** Plan ahead and avoid cash shortages.
**Outcome:** The platform projects future revenue, expenses, and cash balance on a simple visual timeline.
