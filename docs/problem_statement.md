# Problem Statement
- **User:** Small business owner who tracks finances (sales, bills, payroll, budgets) 
in spreadsheets, exported files, or separate tools and has no finance or data background.

# The Problem
- Small business owners need a reliable picture of their finances, but their data is scattered across many sources and is often incomplete or inconsistent. 
Gathering it by hand is slow, and they can't tell whether the numbers are right, so they struggle to see where the business stands now and what's coming next.

# Current Situation
- Owners combine data from multiple spreadsheets and services by hand. 
Errors such as repeated transactions, missing categories, and mixed date formats go unnoticed and can quietly distort totals.

# Impact
- Decisions are made on numbers that may be wrong.
- Overspending and cash shortages are noticed late.
- Time that could go into running the business goes into gathering and checking data.

# Success Metric
- The owner can answer basic questions about their finances, both current and forward-looking, quickly and with confidence in the data.
